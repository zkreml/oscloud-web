Why OSCloud?

No tracking

We do not use cookies on oscloud.cz, so there is nothing to click away. We measure traffic anonymously with our own Umami instance on our own server. Our services only use technical cookies needed to log in, no advertising or tracking cookies. We send no data to Google, Amazon or other advertising and analytics services. There are no ads. Your privacy is the default, not a premium feature.

Open source

Everything we run is open source. Anyone can check the code. No black boxes.

Data in the EU

Our server runs in Germany at Hetzner. Your data does not leave Europe. GDPR is not a marketing trick, it is reality.

A community, not a company

OSCloud is run as a non-profit association. No investors, no exit plan. We do it because we believe it matters.

Basic services are free. You choose Nextcloud by storage size; associations and companies pay extra for invoicing with a company ID, support and more users. All prices are in Czech koruna (CZK).

Prices in the Association and Company columns are totals: the storage price plus the plan surcharge.

Everything can be combined. The Family plan includes a shared folder of the chosen size, and each member also gets 2 GB free. If that is not enough, you can add your own bigger storage at the price from the Individual column; the shared folder stays the same. OSCloud, z. s. is not a VAT payer, all prices are final. Prices are in CZK and you can pay by bank transfer from abroad using our IBAN (account details on the About us page). Your bank converts the amount at its own rate. More about Nextcloud.
Storage larger than 1 TB is possible by arrangement as external storage. The number of users in the Association and Company plans is approximate. For a bigger team, get in touch at info@oscloud.cz and we will work something out.
It is a good idea to back up important data outside OSCloud as well, for example to your own external drive.
