Nextcloud

Your files, calendar and contacts in one place, on servers in Germany. An open source alternative to Google Drive, iCloud or Dropbox, with no ads and no tracking.

Join us Guide in the documentation
  • files available from the web, phone and computer, synchronised between your devices,
  • sharing files and folders by link, optionally with a password and an expiry date,
  • calendar and contacts that sync with your phone,
  • documents, spreadsheets and presentations right in the browser with Collabora Online, even several people at once. Not every Nextcloud provider offers it, but with us it is included,
  • your data stays in the EU, and we keep backups on separate storage.

2 GB free for everyone. You can try Nextcloud for free. When you need more space, add larger storage according to the price list below.

Team for associations and companies

With the association and company plans, only the team lead needs to sign up with OSCloud. They add the other members themselves, you do not have to write to us for each one.

  1. The team lead gets an OSCloud account (request it via the form).
  2. They log in to Nextcloud.
  3. We create a team for your company or association with the agreed storage and make them the team admin.
  4. The team admin adds people to it themselves.

The maximum is 5 users for an association and 10 for a company. If you need more, it is not a problem, just contact support.

How to get started

  1. Fill in the Join us form and tell us what you need (free, or larger storage).
  2. We will e-mail you an invitation and login details.
  3. Log in, set a strong password and turn on two-factor authentication (Settings → Security).

Apps and sync

For comfortable syncing, install the official Nextcloud app. When logging in, enter our server address and your account.

  • Computer (Linux, Windows, macOS): the desktop client syncs selected folders.
  • Android and iOS: the Nextcloud app, optionally with automatic photo upload.
  • Calendar and contacts: via CalDAV and CardDAV, on Android for example with DAVx5.

Detailed steps and screenshots are in the documentation.

Sync is not a backup. If you delete a file on one device, it disappears everywhere else. Back up important things separately and outside OSCloud, for example to your own external drive.

FAQ

How much is free?
Everyone gets 2 GB. Larger storage is from 15 CZK per month, up to 1 TB.
Can I use it for my family?
Yes. The Family plan is for four people in the same household. Everyone has their own account with 2 GB free and all share a 50 GB folder (larger for an extra fee). Plus shared calendars and contacts.
Can I combine plans?
Yes. Family has a shared folder (from 50 GB) and every member gets 2 GB free. Anyone who needs more space just for themselves can buy their own storage at the Individual plan price. The other members can stay on 2 GB free and the shared folder stays the same.
How does Family differ from Individual?
Individual is one account. Family is four separate accounts linked by a shared group and folder. Nextcloud only allows sharing between users within a common group. Without Family you can still send files by link (optionally with a password and expiry), but you cannot share an editable calendar or a shared folder.
Can I use it for an association or a company?
Yes. The association and company plans include more users and an invoice with a company ID.
Where is my data?
On Hetzner servers in Germany, so in the EU.
Can I change my plan?
Write to us and we will adjust the storage.
